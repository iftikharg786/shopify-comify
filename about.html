HOW WE STARTED

Say Goodbye to Toxic Silicone & Ruined Counters

For years, kitchen trivets were an afterthought — flimsy silicone mats that melted, warped cork pads that stained, or bulky metal wire racks that scratched delicate dining tables.

We asked ourselves: What if surface protection could be an architectural centerpiece?

Inspired by Scandinavian minimalism and traditional woodworking joinery, we engineered the Menvel Tree Trivet — an automatic gravity-open mechanism sculpted from 100% solid Black Walnut and Natural Beech wood. When you set cookware down, it expands into a balanced protective star; when lifted, it stands proudly as a handcrafted tree sculpture.

"Every piece of wood has a grain story. We simply shape it so it protects your family's gathering place for generations."
